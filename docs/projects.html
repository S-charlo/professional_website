A Qualitative Analysis of Community Food, Systems, and Care

An qualitative analysis of the roles that agricultural community organizers have in creating a communal food system that operates parallel to a commercial food system in Metro Atlanta. It explores their perceived role in Metro Atlanta food systems and addressing food insecurity.

A Qualitative Analysis of Racial Equity in the Anti-Hunger Workplace

This research was a strategic effort to look at the current state, successes, failures, and possible improvements to the movement to end hunger in the United States. This study interrogated the plans, policies, and strategies of organizational approaches to addressing race in the workplace using survey data collection and semi-structured interviews. It also captured how anti-hunger staff and leadership fueled, experienced, and perceived those efforts.

Conceptual Map Construction

Program Evaluation for the Atlanta Beltline Partnership

This is a mixed-methods formative evalution of the Legacy Residential Retention Program's effect (outcomes and impact) on participants' quality of life, measured by improvements to program participants' financial and emotional well-being. The Legacy Residential Retention Program aims to address displacement cause by redevelopment by paying the difference in property tax increases for eligible residents in Atlanta Beltline neighborhoods.


See the executive summary and infographic produced from the program evaluation findings.

Program Planning: Burke’s Birth & Wellness Mobile Clinic

This plan offers a detailed description of a public health program aimed at bringing mobile maternal care to a maternal care desert. The plan includes a robust justification, a program-specific logic model, a work plan, an evaluation plan, a project timeline, and a budget.

2024 Summer EBT Implementation Report

This mixed-methods study evaluated Summer EBT implementation in Ohio in 2024, and how the program impacted eligible families. Findings were used to generate recommendations for the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (ODEW) and the Ohio Department of Job and Family Services (ODJFS) on how to strengthen the Summer EBT program in future years.

SNAP Employment and Training Program: Best E&T practices for Ohio

The Ohio Department of Jobs and Family Services convened a SNAP E&T Redesign Workgroup in August 2023 alongside approximately 40 stakeholders. Community Solutions was specifically invited to share SNAP E&T best practices from across the country for consideration by the workgroup. After presenting this research to the workgroup, Community Solutions published this paper to share lessons learned with all interested Ohioans to ensure the next generation of Ohio’s SNAP E&T program provides high-quality services and supports to job seekers and employers alike.

The Greene Clothe Collective

Greene Clothe is a Black eco-feminist collective that champions sustainable food production as a praxis of racial, social, and environmental liberation. We co-create mutual-aid, community building, land preservation, and cultural celebration efforts between Black farmers and folks who love the earth via volunteer days, farm tours, and excursions in Atlanta and the Geechee land of Sapelo Island. Our work prioritizes racial justice, farmer solidarity, food abundance, and ecological restoration.

Community Needs Assessment for Emory Recreation & Wellness

The purpose of this community assessment is to gain a deeper understanding of the patterns of participation in group fitness classes among student and non-student members of Emory Recreation and Wellness facilities to generate actionable recommendations for supporting both individual health and community well-being.
